HOW TO PLAY

Guess the word in 6 tries. Each guess must be a valid 5-letter word.

After each guess, the tiles will flip to show how close your guess was:

  • Green — correct letter, correct spot
  • Yellow — correct letter, wrong spot
  • Gray — letter not in the word

Example:

S
T
A
R
E

S is in the word and in the correct spot. A is in the word but in the wrong spot.

THE TWIST: FADING MEMORY

In FADELE, previous guesses fade away after a few turns, forcing you to rely on your memory!

S
T
A
R
E

This is what a faded row looks like — the colors dissolve like fading memories.

The keyboard colors never fade — they are your persistent memory aid. Use them wisely!

A new FADELE is available each day.
